Sources and method

Baseline: CBO’s fiscal 2026 actuals ($7,396B outlays, $5,403B receipts). Option values are rough annual averages, mostly CBO 10-year estimates divided by 10. “Realistic” applies simple behavioral assumptions noted under each item: people work, report, drive or buy less when taxed, and asset prices fall when taxed or dumped. Interest savings from seizures assume about 3.5% on paid-down debt. Per-household figures use about 133 million households. Values are for illustration, not official scores.
